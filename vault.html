    PDF · 171 KB

Mano Atlas

A free, open atlas of mental health in English, built for the Nepali context. Educational resource, not a diagnostic tool: criteria are paraphrased from DSM-5 (2013). Diagnosis belongs to qualified clinicians.

Learning sticks best in small sittings. It is fine to close this tab and come back another day.
