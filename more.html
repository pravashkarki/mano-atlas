What we haven't touched yet: one line each

So the map has no blank spaces: the remaining DSM-5 categories, one honest sentence each. Any of these can become a full chapter later.

CategoryFlagship disordersOne-line handle
Bipolar & relatedBipolar I · II · cyclothymicMania (≥1 wk) defines Bipolar I, with or without depression; hypomania (≥4 d) plus major depression, never mania, defines Bipolar II; see the boundary note on the Depression page
DissociativeDissociative identity · dissociative amnesia · depersonalisation/derealisationDisrupted integration of memory, identity, perception: strongly trauma-linked
Personality disordersCluster A (paranoid, schizoid, schizotypal) · B (antisocial, borderline, histrionic, narcissistic) · C (avoidant, dependent, obsessive-compulsive)Enduring, inflexible patterns of inner experience and behaviour from early adulthood; BPD (instability of relationships, self-image, affect; abandonment fear; self-harm risk) is the one the Mental Health-3 class notes name
NeurocognitiveDelirium · major/mild neurocognitive disorder (Alzheimer's, vascular…)Acquired decline in cognition; delirium is acute, fluctuating, medical: an emergency, not counseling material
Gender dysphoriaGender dysphoriaDistress from incongruence between experienced and assigned gender: the dysphoria, not the identity, is the clinical focus
EliminationEnuresis · encopresisBed-wetting past age 5 / soiling past 4: rule out medical causes, never punish
Sexual dysfunctionsDesire, arousal, orgasm, pain disorders≥6 months, distressing; rarely volunteered in Nepal: ask privately
Paraphilic disordersPedophilic, exhibitionistic, voyeuristic…Atypical arousal is a disorder only when it causes distress/impairment or involves non-consent
Key points
  1. DSM-5 has about twenty chapters; this atlas covers ten in depth and the rest in one honest sentence each.
  2. Bipolar I is defined by mania; bipolar II by hypomania plus depression; delirium is a medical emergency, not a counselling case.
  3. Knowing a category exists is enough to refer well; any of these can become a full chapter later.
