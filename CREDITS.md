# Credits and sources

One file recording every third-party input to Mano Atlas, who made it, and
what terms apply to it. The licence in `LICENSE` covers only what we wrote.
Everything named here belongs to someone else and keeps its own terms.

Where a permission has not been obtained, that is stated plainly rather than
implied. Nothing on this page should be read as a grant we are not able to
make.

Reader-facing version of this file: <https://manoatlas.com/terms>

## The instructors

This site grew out of the CTEVT Psychosocial Counselor course, and it could
not exist without the people who wrote and taught that material. They wrote
the teaching text, the session structures, the skill drills, the case material
and the exam terms that these pages adapt. We are grateful.

| Contributor | What they contributed | What we did with it | Permission |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunil Parajuli** | Four decks: Sub module 1 (foundations, the goals table, Greek roots of psychology, Engel, the ethics ladder); Applying Verbal Communication Skill (the five listening and four moving skills); Counselling Approaches (psychodynamic, behavioural, humanistic, CBT); Counselling Process Submodule 6 (the counselling stages, SMART goals, termination criteria, the first-session scripts) | Adapted across `roots`, `approaches`, `basics`, `ethics`, `skills-listening`, `skills-moving`, `process`, `first-sessions`, and links on `suicide`, `psychosis`, `tools` | **Not yet contacted** |

| Not attributed on the deck | Four decks: SUB module 2 New (the psychosocial rings, elements, pyramid, CB-MHPSS principles); Mental Health 3 (the severity-by-support table, the workforce, the national context); NON-Verbal Communication Skills (SOLER, the three functions, the four domains); Basic Therapy Techniques (the ERP principles and hierarchy, behavioural activation, ACT) | Adapted across `wellbeing`, `basics`, `nepal`, `skills`, `techniques`, and disorder chapters | **Cannot be asked: no author named on the deck** |

## Data and statistics

verification state of every figure is tracked in `review/data-ledger.md`.

identification only, under the applicable guidelines.

## Works we name but do not reproduce

Books, films, clinical guidelines and public documents are named by title in
the Learn more sections rather than quoted. Titles and authors are facts; the
text is not ours to copy.

## Corrections

If something here is wrong, incomplete, or your name is on the wrong row, tell
us and we will fix it. The address is in the footer of every page. If a
message says a child is being harmed, we have to tell the police, so that kind
of message cannot be kept private. The notice is on the terms page.
