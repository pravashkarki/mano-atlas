These are what a trained counselor does with a person over several sessions, laid out in order so a student can see the shape of each. If you are here for yourself or for someone you love, the skills to try at home are the "A skill to try" boxes on each disorder page; this page shows what the counselor's side of the work looks like.

यी प्रशिक्षित परामर्शकर्ताले कुनै व्यक्तिसँग धेरै सत्रमा गर्ने काम हुन्, विद्यार्थीले हरेकको आकार देखून् भनेर क्रमैसँग राखिएका। आफ्ना लागि वा प्रियजनका लागि आउनुभएको हो भने घरमा अभ्यास गर्ने सीप हरेक विकार-पृष्ठको «अभ्यास गर्ने सीप» बाकसमा छन्; यो पृष्ठले परामर्शकर्ताको तर्फको काम कस्तो देखिन्छ, त्यो देखाउँछ।

CTEVT PSC curriculum: on the official Psychosocial Counselor syllabus, can appear in the exam. Beyond curriculum: added for completeness, not examinable. A chip like 296.2x · F32 is the diagnosis code: the DSM-5's older ICD-9-CM number, then the ICD-10 "F-code" used in hospital records. Non-students can ignore all of these.

सीटीईभीटी पाठ्यक्रम: आधिकारिक मनोसामाजिक परामर्शकर्ता पाठ्यक्रमभित्र, परीक्षामा आउन सक्ने। पाठ्यक्रमभन्दा बाहिर: पूर्णताका लागि थपिएको, परीक्षामा नआउने। 296.2x · F32 जस्तो चिप निदान-कोड हो: DSM-5 को पुरानो ICD-9-CM नम्बर, अनि अस्पतालको रेकर्डमा प्रयोग हुने ICD-10 «एफ-कोड»। विद्यार्थी नभएकाले यी बेवास्ता गरे हुन्छ।

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), developed by Steven Hayes, starts from one idea: the problem is rarely the anxious feeling itself, it is the struggle to get rid of it. The struggle shrinks a life. ACT works through six processes, usually woven together rather than done in order:

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), स्टिभन हेजले विकास गरेको, एउटा विचारबाट सुरु हुन्छ: समस्या प्रायः चिन्ताको भावना आफैं होइन, त्यसलाई हटाउने सङ्घर्ष हो। सङ्घर्षले जीवन साँघुरो पार्छ। ACT छ प्रक्रियाबाट चल्छ, प्रायः क्रमैसँग होइन, सँगसँगै बुनिएर:

  • Acceptance: let the feeling be there instead of fighting it.
  • Defusion: see a thought as words in the mind, not as a fact.
  • Present moment: attention on what is happening now, not on what might happen.
  • Self as context: you are the one noticing the thoughts, not the thoughts themselves.
  • Values: what kind of person, parent, worker, friend the client wants to be.
  • Committed action: small steps in that direction, taken with the anxiety still present.
  • स्वीकार (acceptance): भावनासँग लड्नुको सट्टा त्यसलाई त्यहीँ रहन दिने।
  • डिफ्युजन (defusion): विचारलाई तथ्य होइन, दिमागमा आएका शब्दका रूपमा हेर्ने।
  • वर्तमान क्षण: के हुन सक्छ भन्नेमा होइन, अहिले के भइरहेको छ त्यसमा ध्यान।
  • साक्षी-आत्म (self as context): तपाईं विचार होइन, विचार देख्ने व्यक्ति हो।
  • मूल्य (values): सेवाग्राही कस्तो व्यक्ति, अभिभावक, कर्मचारी, साथी बन्न चाहन्छ।
  • प्रतिबद्ध कार्य: चिन्ता सँगै रहँदारहँदै त्यही दिशामा साना पाइला।

One worked illustration (fictional): a young woman who skips meetings at work because she is afraid of being judged. The class notes teach it as nine steps; a real course of sessions loops back and forth between them.

एउटा काल्पनिक उदाहरण: अरूले के सोच्लान् भन्ने डरले अफिसका मिटिङ छल्ने एक युवती। कक्षा-नोटले यसलाई नौ चरणमा सिकाउँछ; वास्तविक सत्रहरू यी चरणबीच अघि-पछि गरिरहन्छन्।

  1. Rapport and the pattern. "I want to understand what you are going through. There is no judgement here." Ask what she does when the nervousness comes. The answer, "I skip the meeting", shows the loop: anxiety, avoid, short relief, more anxiety next time.
  2. Explain it simply. "Anxiety is normal. The problem is not the anxiety, it is running from it." The picture: quicksand, the harder you struggle, the deeper you sink.
  3. Acceptance. "Instead of fighting the anxiety, let us learn to make space for it." Where is it in the body? "In my chest." "Let it be there. You do not have to remove it." (Eyes open is fine; closed eyes are optional.)
  4. Defusion. "Thoughts are not facts. They are words in your mind." The thought "I will embarrass myself" becomes "I am having the thought that I will embarrass myself." Said aloud many times, the sentence loses its grip.
  5. Present moment. "Let us focus on what is happening right now." The 5-4-3-2-1 grounding from page 22, used in the minutes before a meeting.
  6. Values. "What kind of person do you want to be?" "I want to grow in my work." "Is skipping meetings helping you grow?"
  7. Committed action. "You can feel anxious and still act." A ladder of small steps: say one sentence, ask one question, share one idea.
  8. Practice between sessions. "Practice matters. Small steps make the change." Attend one meeting; say the defusion sentence once a day.
  9. Review. "It is all right if the anxiety is still there. What matters is what you do." The expected outcome is honest: the anxiety may remain, the behaviour changes, confidence follows.
  1. सम्बन्ध र ढाँचा। «म तपाईंको अनुभव बुझ्न चाहन्छु। यहाँ कसैले जाँच्दैन।» नर्भस हुँदा के गर्नुहुन्छ भनेर सोध्ने। «मिटिङ छल्छु» भन्ने जवाफले चक्र देखाउँछ: चिन्ता, छल्ने, छोटो राहत, अर्को पटक झन् चिन्ता।
  2. सरल गरी बुझाउने। «चिन्ता हुनु सामान्य हो। समस्या चिन्ता होइन, त्यसबाट भाग्नु हो।» चित्र: दलदल, जति छटपटायो उति डुबिन्छ।
  3. स्वीकार। «चिन्तासँग लड्नुको सट्टा त्यसलाई ठाउँ दिन सिकौं।» शरीरमा कहाँ छ? «छातीमा।» «त्यहीँ रहन दिनुहोस्। हटाउनैपर्छ भन्ने छैन।» (आँखा खुला राखे हुन्छ; चिम्लनु ऐच्छिक हो।)
  4. डिफ्युजन। «विचार तथ्य होइनन्। ती दिमागमा आएका शब्द हुन्।» «म लाजमर्दो हुनेछु» भन्ने विचार «मलाई म लाजमर्दो हुनेछु भन्ने विचार आइरहेको छ» बन्छ। धेरै पटक ठूलो स्वरले भन्दा वाक्यको पकड खुकुलिन्छ।
  5. वर्तमान क्षण। «अहिले के भइरहेको छ, त्यसमा ध्यान दिऔं।» पृष्ठ २२ को ५-४-३-२-१ ग्राउन्डिङ, मिटिङअघिका मिनेटमा।
  6. मूल्य। «तपाईं कस्तो व्यक्ति बन्न चाहनुहुन्छ?» «काममा अघि बढ्न चाहन्छु।» «मिटिङ छल्नुले अघि बढ्न सघाइरहेको छ?»
  7. प्रतिबद्ध कार्य। «चिन्ता भए पनि तपाईं काम गर्न सक्नुहुन्छ।» साना पाइलाको भर्‍याङ: एउटा वाक्य बोल्ने, एउटा प्रश्न सोध्ने, एउटा विचार राख्ने।
  8. सत्रबीचको अभ्यास। «अभ्यास महत्त्वपूर्ण छ। साना कदमले परिवर्तन ल्याउँछ।» एउटा मिटिङमा बस्ने; डिफ्युजन-वाक्य दिनमा एक पटक भन्ने।
  9. समीक्षा। «चिन्ता अझै भए पनि ठीक छ। महत्त्वपूर्ण कुरा तपाईंले के गर्नुहुन्छ भन्ने हो।» अपेक्षित नतिजा इमानदार छ: चिन्ता रहन सक्छ, व्यवहार बदलिन्छ, आत्मविश्वास पछि आउँछ।

Where it is used: anxiety disorders, depression and OCD have the best evidence. For trauma and substance use it is an adjunct, with a trained provider, not a stand-alone answer.

कहाँ प्रयोग हुन्छ: चिन्ता-विकार, डिप्रेसन र ओसीडीमा सबैभन्दा राम्रो प्रमाण छ। आघात र लागुपदार्थमा यो प्रशिक्षित सेवाप्रदायकसँगको सहायक विधि हो, एक्लो उत्तर होइन।

Depression makes a person do less; doing less makes the mood worse; the worse mood makes them do even less. That is the spiral on page 08. Behavioural activation breaks it at the easiest link to move: the activity. The one line to remember is action before motivation: the client acts first, and the wish to act returns later. The class notes teach nine steps.

डिप्रेसनले मान्छेलाई कम गर्न लगाउँछ; कम गर्दा मन झन् खराब हुन्छ; खराब मनले झन् कम गराउँछ। त्यही पृष्ठ ०८ को भुमरी हो। व्यवहार-सक्रियताले सबैभन्दा हल्लाउन सजिलो कडी, काम, समातेर भुमरी तोड्छ। सम्झने एउटै लाइन: मन लाग्नुअघि नै काम: सेवाग्राहीले पहिले गर्छ, गर्ने इच्छा पछि फर्कन्छ। कक्षा-नोटले नौ चरण सिकाउँछ।

  1. Understand the day. Sleep, meals, who they talk to, what they have stopped doing.
  2. Explain the cycle. "When we do less we feel worse, and when we feel worse we do less. We break it with action."
  3. Monitor activity. For a few days the client writes what they did each hour and how they felt.
  4. Schedule activity. A daily plan of small, realistic tasks. Not perfection, movement.
  5. Activate. Do the planned thing even when it does not feel like it. This step is the therapy.
  6. Add pleasure and achievement. One thing that is enjoyable, one that gives a sense of having done something.
  7. Solve the barriers. Tiredness, fear, no support: each named and worked around.
  8. Practise between sessions. This is where the change actually happens.
  9. Review. Every small gain counted out loud.

The chart is the tool. How to introduce it: "This chart helps us see how what you do affects how you feel. Nobody is judging you; we are learning what works." Activities fall into four kinds: routine (bathing, eating, sleeping), pleasure (music, a film), achievement (cleaning, finishing a task), social (talking to family or friends).

A chip like 296.2x · F32 is the diagnosis code: the DSM-5's older ICD-9-CM number, then the ICD-10 "F-code" used in hospital records. Non-students can ignore all of these.

सीटीईभीटी पाठ्यक्रम: आधिकारिक मनोसामाजिक परामर्शकर्ता पाठ्यक्रमभित्र, परीक्षामा आउन सक्ने। पाठ्यक्रमभन्दा बाहिर: पूर्णताका लागि थपिएको, परीक्षामा नआउने। 296.2x · F32 जस्तो चिप निदान-कोड हो: DSM-5 को पुरानो ICD-9-CM नम्बर, अनि अस्पतालको रेकर्डमा प्रयोग हुने ICD-10 «एफ-कोड»। विद्यार्थी नभएकाले यी बेवास्ता गरे हुन्छ।

Exposure and response prevention (ERP) is the behavioural treatment for OCD. It rests on the loop drawn on page 07: trigger, obsession, anxiety, compulsion, short relief, and the loop tightened for next time. ERP breaks it at the compulsion. This is a treatment delivered by a trained person over weeks, with consent at every step; what follows is the shape of it, not a kit to run alone.

एक्सपोजर र प्रतिक्रिया-रोकथाम (ईआरपी) ओसीडीको व्यवहारपरक उपचार हो। यो पृष्ठ ०७ मा कोरिएको चक्रमा अडिएको छ: ट्रिगर, अब्सेसन, चिन्ता, कम्पल्सन, छोटो राहत, अनि अर्को पटकका लागि झन् कसिएको चक्र। ईआरपीले चक्र कम्पल्सनमै तोड्छ। यो प्रशिक्षित व्यक्तिले हप्तौंसम्म, हरेक चरणमा सहमति लिएर गर्ने उपचार हो; तल यसको आकार छ, एक्लै चलाउने किट होइन।

First, three things the client hears: OCD is frightening, not dangerous; a thought is not a fact; the ritual calms for a minute and feeds the problem for years.

पहिले, सेवाग्राहीले सुन्ने तीन कुरा: ओसीडी डरलाग्दो हो, खतरनाक होइन; विचार तथ्य होइन; रिचुअलले एक मिनेट शान्त पार्छ र वर्षौं समस्या खुवाउँछ।

Then a ladder. Together they list feared situations from easiest to hardest and rate each on SUDS, the 0 to 10 distress scale from page 22 (some services use 0 to 100; the idea is the same). Example for a fear of contamination:

The session. Start on the lowest rung. The client touches the table and stays with the feeling: "Stay with the anxiety. Do not wash your hands yet." "Notice the thoughts without acting on them." The aim is not to make the anxiety fall; it is to stay long enough to learn that the feared outcome does not follow, and that the anxiety, whether it falls or not, is survivable. Slow breathing may steady the body, but it is not used as an escape hatch from the feeling. SUDS is noted before, during and after, so the client can see their own record over the weeks.

सत्र। सबैभन्दा तल्लो खुड्किलोबाट सुरु। सेवाग्राही टेबल छुन्छ र भावनासँगै बस्छ: «चिन्तासँगै बस्नुहोस्। अहिले हात नधुनुहोस्।» «विचारलाई नमानी, हेरेर मात्र बस्नुहोस्।» लक्ष्य चिन्ता घटाउनु होइन; डराएको कुरा हुँदैन रहेछ, र चिन्ता घटोस् वा नघटोस्, सहन सकिन्छ भन्ने सिक्न पुग्ने बेरसम्म बस्नु हो। बिस्तारै सासले शरीर स्थिर पार्न सक्छ, तर भावनाबाट भाग्ने ढोकाका रूपमा प्रयोग गरिँदैन। SUDS अघि, बीच र पछि लेखिन्छ, ताकि हप्तौंको आफ्नै अभिलेख सेवाग्राहीले देखोस्।

Response prevention is the principle that makes it ERP rather than just exposure: after the trigger, no washing, no sanitiser, no avoiding, or at first a delay that lengthens each week (ten minutes, then thirty, then an hour). The same rung is repeated until it is boring, and only then does the ladder move up.

प्रतिक्रिया-रोकथाम त्यो सिद्धान्त हो जसले यसलाई एक्सपोजर मात्र होइन, ईआरपी बनाउँछ: ट्रिगरपछि हात नधुने, स्यानिटाइजर नलगाउने, नछल्ने, वा सुरुमा हरेक हप्ता लम्बिँदै जाने ढिलाइ (दस मिनेट, अनि तीस, अनि एक घण्टा)। एउटै खुड्किलो झ्याउ नलागुन्जेल दोहोर्‍याइन्छ, अनि मात्र भर्‍याङ माथि सर्छ।

For the counselor: never rush; always start low; consent and readiness before every rung; support the person warmly but do not reassure them about the fear itself ("you will be fine" is a ritual by proxy); keep the record. Alongside, gently test the thought: "If I do not wash I will get sick" becomes "I can stay safe without washing at once; I can live with not knowing." Expect a stray intrusive thought to return now and then after treatment; the plan is not to go back to the ritual, and to keep the practice going.

परामर्शकर्ताका लागि: हतार कहिल्यै नगर्ने; सधैं तलबाट सुरु; हरेक खुड्किलोअघि सहमति र तयारी; व्यक्तिलाई न्यानो साथ दिने तर डरकै बारेमा ढुक्क नपार्ने («केही हुँदैन» भन्नु अरूमार्फतको रिचुअल हो); अभिलेख राख्ने। सँगसँगै विचारलाई नरम गरी जाँच्ने: «नधोए बिरामी हुन्छु» बाट «तुरुन्त नधोए पनि म सुरक्षित रहन सक्छु; नजानेरै पनि बाँच्न सक्छु।» उपचारपछि पनि कहिलेकाहीँ घुसपैठ-विचार फर्कन सक्छ भन्ने अपेक्षा राख्ने; योजना रिचुअलमा नफर्कने र अभ्यास जारी राख्ने हो।

A skill to try · three minutesअभ्यास गर्ने सीप · तीन मिनेट

Five steps through a strong feeling. Notice early: feelings climb like a traffic light, green, yellow, red; catch it at yellow, the way you catch milk before it boils over. Your body gives the signal first: faster heart, quicker breath, a tight jaw. Find it: eyes open is fine; where does it sit, shoulders, chest, jaw? Let that place soften a little. Name it: anger is often a shield; ask "is this anger, or is there hurt, fear or shame underneath?" A named feeling loses some of its force. Breathe out long: in through the nose, out slowly and a little longer than in; no holding, no counting contest, stop if it feels wrong. Ask what it needs: turn it into an I-message: "I feel ___ because I needed ___; next time, could you ___?" Later, if it helps, write down what happened, what you felt and what you needed, facts first, blame left out.

तीव्र भावनाबाट पाँच पाइलामा। चाँडै थाहा पाउने: भावना ट्राफिक बत्तीजस्तै चढ्छ, हरियो, पहेँलो, रातो; पहेँलोमै समात्ने, उम्लन लागेको दूध पोखिनुअघि समातेजस्तै। शरीरले पहिले सङ्केत दिन्छ: मुटु छिटो, सास छिटो, बङ्गारा कसिएको। खोज्ने: आँखा खुला राखे हुन्छ; कहाँ बसेको छ, काँध, छाती, बङ्गारा? त्यो ठाउँ अलिकति खुकुलो हुन दिने। नाम दिने: रिस प्रायः ढाल हो; सोध्ने, «यो रिस हो कि भित्र चोट, डर वा लाज छ?» नाम पाएको भावनाले केही शक्ति गुमाउँछ। लामो सास छोड्ने: नाकबाट तान्ने, बिस्तारै र तान्दाभन्दा अलि लामो छोड्ने; सास नरोक्ने, गन्तीको प्रतिस्पर्धा नगर्ने, असजिलो लागे रोक्ने। के चाहिएको हो सोध्ने: म-वाक्यमा ढाल्ने: «मलाई ___ लाग्यो किनभने मलाई ___ चाहिएको थियो; अर्को पटक ___ गर्न सकिन्छ?» पछि, काम लाग्छ भने, के भयो, के लाग्यो, के चाहिएको थियो लेख्ने, पहिले तथ्य, दोष छोडेर।

Key pointsमुख्य बुँदा
  1. ACT does not remove the anxiety; it loosens the struggle with it and moves the person toward what they value, one small committed step at a time.
  2. Behavioural activation runs on one rule, action before motivation: the activity chart shows the client, in their own handwriting, that what they do moves their mood.
  3. ERP faces the trigger and skips the ritual; the point of staying is to learn the feared outcome does not follow, not to force the anxiety down. Never reassure about the fear itself.
  1. ACT ले चिन्ता हटाउँदैन; त्यससँगको सङ्घर्ष खुकुलो पार्छ र व्यक्तिलाई आफ्नो मूल्यतिर, एक-एक सानो प्रतिबद्ध पाइलामा, बढाउँछ।
  2. व्यवहार-सक्रियता एउटै नियममा चल्छ, मन लाग्नुअघि नै काम: गतिविधि-चार्टले सेवाग्राहीलाई आफ्नै हातको लेखाइमा देखाउँछ कि काम गर्दा मन चल्छ।
  3. ईआरपीले ट्रिगरको सामना गर्छ र रिचुअल छोड्छ; बस्नुको अर्थ डराएको कुरा हुँदैन रहेछ भन्ने सिक्नु हो, चिन्तालाई जबरजस्ती घटाउनु होइन। डरकै बारेमा कहिल्यै ढुक्क नपार्ने।
