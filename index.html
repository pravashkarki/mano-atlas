01

The DSM-5 map, where everything livesDSM-5 नक्सा, के कहाँ पर्छ

DSM-5 organises mental disorders into about twenty chapters, ordered roughly by the age at which they typically first appear. Colour marks the categories this atlas covers in depth; the tag on each cell shows whether it sits inside the CTEVT PSC curriculum.

DSM-5 ले मानसिक विकारहरूलाई करिब बीस अध्यायमा बाँड्छ, प्रायः जुन उमेरमा पहिलो पटक देखा पर्छ, त्यही क्रममा। रङले यस एट्लासले गहिराइमा समेट्ने वर्गहरू देखाउँछ; प्रत्येक कोठाको चिन्हले त्यो सीटीईभीटी पाठ्यक्रमभित्र पर्छ कि पर्दैन भन्ने देखाउँछ।

On every disorder page a small chip like 296.2x · F32 sits beside the heading: these are the diagnosis codes, the DSM-5's older ICD-9-CM number first, then the ICD-10 code (the "F-code") used in hospital records, referral letters and insurance forms today; the DSM-5 prints the US clinical modification, ICD-10-CM, of the same code. The x stands for a digit that encodes severity.

हरेक विकार-पृष्ठको शीर्षकछेउ 296.2x · F32 जस्तो सानो चिप छ: यी निदान-कोड हुन्, पहिले DSM-5 को पुरानो ICD-9-CM नम्बर, अनि आजकल अस्पतालको रेकर्ड, रेफरल-पत्र र बिमा-फारममा प्रयोग हुने ICD-10 कोड («एफ-कोड»); DSM-5 ले त्यही कोडको अमेरिकी रूप ICD-10-CM छाप्छ। x भनेको गम्भीरता जनाउने अङ्क हो।

For CTEVT PSC exam takers: if a paper asks you to list the types of mental illness, give the curriculum's own answer first; anxiety, depression, conversion disorder, psychosis, plus child mental illness, and only then add breadth. Breadth after accuracy, never instead of it.
