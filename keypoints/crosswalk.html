Key pointsमुख्य बुँदा
  1. Every module and sub-module of the PSC course is listed in the course map above, each with an honest status: covered here, partly, or coming soon.
  2. The course is 780 hours and 600 marks; 500 of the 600 marks are practical, so practising skills beats memorising.
  3. The atlas reads like a book, the curriculum is a job: same syllabus, different order. The map marks every topic on the site and every gap honestly.
