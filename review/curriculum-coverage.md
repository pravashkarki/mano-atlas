# Mano Atlas vs CTEVT PSC curriculum: unit-by-unit coverage

Checked 2026-09-11 (read-only audit, part of the source inventory in `intake.md`). Source: `curriculum.pdf` in the Drive folder, 56 pages. Site: `content/*.html`, English text.

Unit = one curriculum "Task" row (the level at which the curriculum lists related technical knowledge). Sub-topics noted where they decide the status.

- COVERED: taught on the site.
- PARTLY: named or touched, not taught at curriculum depth (a one-clause or one-table-row mention counts as PARTLY).
- MISSING: not on the site.
- OUT OF SCOPE: course logistics, not content for a public reading site.

## 1. The curriculum

- Title: Curriculum for Psychosocial Counselor [PSC]. Issuer: CTEVT, Curriculum Development Division, Sanothimi, Bhaktapur. Year: 2010. Competency-based (DACUM).
- Duration: 780 hours over 6 months = 620 in-house + 160 OJT. 20% theory / 80% task performance. Entry: +2 pass, Nepali citizen, 21+, entrance exam. Pass: 60% average; 90% attendance.
- Marks: 600 total (Th 100 / Pr 500).

| Module (official numbering) | Sub-modules (hours Th+Pr=Tot) | Hours | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Psychosocial Intervention | 1 Wellbeing, Intervention & CBI 10+10=20 · 2 Mental Health 24+10=34 · 3 (Gender,) Culture & Counseling 18+6=24 · 4 Human Development & Behavior 14+4=18 · 5 Special Issues 45+24=69 | 165 | 100 |
| 2 Case Management | 1 Managing Counseling Center 8+4=12 · 2 Documentation 6+6=12 · 3 Supervision 6+12=18 · 4 Linkage, Coordination & Referrals 6+12=18 | 60 | 150 |
| 3 Counseling Skill and Process | 1 Approaches 18+12=30 · 2 Types/forms 25+18=43 · 3 Basics 8+6=14 · 4 Communication Skills 24+75=99 · 5 Counseling Process 36+46=82 · 6 Alternative Tools 18+36=54 | 322 | 200 |
| 4 Capacity Building | 1 Facilitation Skill 12+47=59 · 2 Professionalism Development 8+8=16 | 73 | 50 |
| OJT | practical | 160 | 100 |

Source inconsistencies worth knowing:

- The TOC and the course-structure table use the numbering above. The detail pages (pp. 11-12, 25) print "Module 1: Counseling Skills and Process" (with Wellbeing/CBI as its SM1) and "Module 3: Psychosocial Intervention"; p. 11 calls Psychosocial Intervention "Module 2". The site's official numbering follows the course-structure table, which is the right call, but the site's pages mix the two (see crosswalk errors).
- The arithmetic doesn't add up everywhere: M3 Th 129 + Pr 191 = 320, not 322 (the sub-module Pr values sum to 193); M4 sub-modules sum to 75, not 73; theory subtotal 284 vs 286 by sum. The 620/780 totals still hold.
- Special Issues task 5 is blank; its objectives list "medically unexplained somatic complaints" twice.

## 10. Crosswalk errors (content/crosswalk.html and related)

1. It is not a curriculum-to-page map. It's a disorder-level study-status table ("Studied", "To study: curriculum", "Roadmap Month 2 chapter", "Handbook Vol 1, Ch 1") that points at a private PSC study handbook and roadmap, neither of which is on the site.
2. Its statuses are stale. Depression, conversion/somatic, psychosis, child, PTSD, PFA, substance, suicide, GBV, culture and human development are all live chapters, yet marked "To study" with a roadmap month.
3. Rows for amygdala, OCD, BDD, eating, sleep and selective mutism are beyond-curriculum topics presented inside a curriculum map, with no label saying so.
