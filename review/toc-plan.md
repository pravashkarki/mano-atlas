Status: DRAFT for pair review, then the site owner's approval. Nothing on the site changes from this file. It is the map; chapters are built as we go, as decks arrive or research is done.

Inputs: the 34 live pages (`build.py` PAGES), the curriculum audit (`review/curriculum-coverage.md`, 159 task units), the beyond-curriculum research (`review/beyond-curriculum.md`, 65 topics, IDs like A2, F3), the source inventory (`review/intake.md`).

## How to read it

Each line: position · slug · title · status · anchor · note.

- **LIVE**: on the site, stays as is.
- **LIVE+**: on the site, gets new material (curriculum gap or research), same slug.
- **NEW-C**: new chapter for a curriculum gap. Likely fed by an instructor deck still to come; research fills in if no deck arrives.
- **NEW-R**: new chapter from our own research (beyond the curriculum).
- Anchor: curriculum unit (M = module, SM = sub-module, T = task; official numbering) and/or research ID.
- OPTIONAL research topics are never chapters: they become a "Go deeper" expander or a card inside the chapter named (complexity is opt-in).

Rules this draft follows: one reader question per chapter, about 1800 English words, split beyond that; new chapters go where a first-time reader needs them; live slugs never change (numbers follow position); the crosswalk, not the reading order, is the curriculum view.
