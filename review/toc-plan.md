## How to read it

Each line: position · slug · title · status · anchor · note.

- **LIVE**: on the site, stays as is.
- **LIVE+**: on the site, gets new material, same slug.
- **NEW-C**: new chapter for a curriculum gap.
- **NEW-R**: new chapter from our own research (beyond the curriculum).
- Anchor: curriculum unit (M = module, SM = sub-module, T = task; official numbering) and/or research ID.
- OPTIONAL research topics are never chapters: they become a "Go deeper" expander or a card inside the chapter named.

Rules: one reader question per chapter, about 1800 English words, split beyond that; new chapters go where a first-time reader needs them; live slugs never change (numbers follow position); the crosswalk, not the reading order, is the curriculum view.

## Scope rules for public pages

1. medicines: observation, adherence support and referral only; no doses, no "what to take"; a scope banner at the top.
2. brief-therapies: what each intervention is, who it is for, the training and supervision it requires; never a self-run protocol.
3. trauma-support and creative: stabilisation, supportive counselling, safe cognitive work and referral; no trauma-memory processing steps; narrative exposure as a concept that needs training.
4. personality: emotion dysregulation as a spectrum with skills; a label only from a qualified diagnostician; never a self-test.
5. Every chapter that names a practice from the not-recommended page says why inline and links there.
