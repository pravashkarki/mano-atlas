## Source cautions (verify before any of this ships)
- Drop "60 to 80% of communication is non-verbal": this is the Mehrabian misquote; do not import a figure.
- Drop "conscious mind is 10%": folklore, no source.
- Seating distance: decks conflict (2 to 4 ft vs 3 to 5 ft). Import no number; say "about an arm's length or more, tuned to the person and culture".
- Id/ego/superego "personality types" and psychosexual fixations: present as the classroom framing of a historical theory, one sentence that they are not diagnostic or evidence-based. Same for Oedipus/Electra.
- Little Albert: history, with an ethics line (would not pass an ethics board today; Albert was never deconditioned).
- Rogers: "three core conditions" is fine; Rogers 1957 listed six necessary and sufficient conditions. One clause.
- Maslow: five tiers is the 1943 model; fine for the curriculum.
- ACT founder Hayes, ERP under CBT, Beck/Ellis, Egan SOLER: all check out.
- ERP and ACT are counselor protocols done with a trained person; the chapter says so at the top. Skill boxes stay skills.
- Body scan appears in ACT step 3 and emotion-processing step 2: allowed (the house rule bans it on the eating page only). Offer eyes-open version for trauma clients, as tools.html already does.
- Deck typo "Almighty/Amygdala": ignore; use amygdala only.
- SUDS scale: site uses 0 to 10; deck uses 0 to 100. Keep 0 to 10, note both.
- The Verbal Skills deck's Nepali extracts garbled (font encoding). Every Nepali phrase must be read from the rendered page (render with a small Swift PDFKit script or install poppler), never from the extracted text.

## Six-month content strategy (new decks arrive weekly until about March 2027)

This batch is the first of roughly 25 weekly intakes. The per-batch plan above is fine once, but three things in the current build make every future insert cost more than it should. Fix them in this batch, before the two inserts, so the next 24 are cheap.

### 1. Cross-references by slug, not by number
Today the text says "page 20" and `link_page_refs()` turns it into a link. Every insert renumbers pages and silently breaks the prose ("page 20" now points at the wrong chapter). Change: authors write `{{page:tools}}` (Nepali `{{पृष्ठ:tools}}`); the build resolves it to the current number in the right script and links it. One-time migration of the 14 existing refs. After this, inserting a chapter touches only PAGES.

### 2. Numbers derived from order
PAGES carries explicit "01".."30". Change: drop the column; the build numbers pages by position inside the list. Inserting a row anywhere renumbers everything consistently, including Devanagari numerals, ghost numerals, pagers, sitemap, search index and the crosswalk range (which becomes a computed span, "16 to {{last:practice}}").

### 3. An intake ledger, one row per deck
`review/intake.md` (git-ignored like the rest of review/): deck · date · pages · where each part landed (file + section) · what was dropped and why · open conflicts with the site. This plan is the first entry. It is the audit trail the CLAUDE.md "content truth status" paragraph needs, and it stops a deck being mined twice.

### Rules of growth, so the site stays one mind
- Chapter ceiling: about 1800 English words. Past that, split by reader question, do not append. Depth goes into closed-by-default "Go deeper" expanders (complexity is opt-in).
- Position rule: a new chapter goes where a first-time reader needs it in the order, never at the end of a group because that is cheaper.
- Group rule: the sidebar has four groups and the practice group is already 11 chapters; at about 16 it needs sub-headings that follow the CTEVT modules (1 Intervention · 2 Case management · 3 Skill and process · 4 Capacity building). Plan for that split around intake 6 to 8, not now.
- Every intake ships with: both languages, keypoints, quiz, crosswalk row, glossary check, search aliases, light and dark screenshots.
- Source cautions are decided per deck in the ledger and applied once; a claim rejected in one deck is not re-imported from the next.
- Frequency target: one deck per week, one commit per chapter, live the same day. Bigger decks (the 117-page Process deck) split across two weeks by stage rather than shipping half-finished.
