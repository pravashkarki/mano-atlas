Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), developed by Steven Hayes, starts from one idea: the problem is rarely the anxious feeling itself, it is the struggle to get rid of it. The struggle shrinks a life. ACT works through six processes, usually woven together rather than done in order:

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), स्टिभन हेजले विकास गरेको, एउटा विचारबाट सुरु हुन्छ: समस्या प्रायः चिन्ताको भावना आफैं होइन, त्यसलाई हटाउने सङ्घर्ष हो। सङ्घर्षले जीवन साँघुरो पार्छ। ACT छ प्रक्रियाबाट चल्छ, प्रायः क्रमैसँग होइन, सँगसँगै बुनिएर:

  • Acceptance: let the feeling be there instead of fighting it.
  • Defusion: see a thought as words in the mind, not as a fact.
  • Present moment: attention on what is happening now, not on what might happen.
  • Self as context: you are the one noticing the thoughts, not the thoughts themselves.
  • Values: what kind of person, parent, worker, friend the client wants to be.
  • Committed action: small steps in that direction, taken with the anxiety still present.
  • स्वीकार (acceptance): भावनासँग लड्नुको सट्टा त्यसलाई त्यहीँ रहन दिने।
  • डिफ्युजन (defusion): विचारलाई तथ्य होइन, दिमागमा आएका शब्दका रूपमा हेर्ने।
  • वर्तमान क्षण: के हुन सक्छ भन्नेमा होइन, अहिले के भइरहेको छ त्यसमा ध्यान।
  • Self as context: तपाईं विचार होइन, विचार देख्ने व्यक्ति हो।
  • मूल्य (values): सेवाग्राही कस्तो व्यक्ति, अभिभावक, कर्मचारी, साथी बन्न चाहन्छ।
  • प्रतिबद्ध कार्य: चिन्ता सँगै रहँदारहँदै त्यही दिशामा साना पाइला।

One worked illustration (fictional): a young woman who skips meetings at work because she is afraid of being judged. The class notes teach it as nine steps; a real course of sessions loops back and forth between them.

एउटा काल्पनिक उदाहरण: अरूले के सोच्लान् भन्ने डरले अफिसका मिटिङ छल्ने एक युवती। कक्षा-नोटले यसलाई नौ चरणमा सिकाउँछ; वास्तविक सत्रहरू यी चरणबीच अघि-पछि गरिरहन्छन्।

  1. Rapport and the pattern. "I want to understand what you are going through. There is no judgement here." Ask what she does when the nervousness comes. The answer, "I skip the meeting", shows the loop: anxiety, avoid, short relief, more anxiety next time.
  2. Explain it simply. "Anxiety is normal. The problem is not the anxiety, it is running from it." The picture: quicksand, the harder you struggle, the deeper you sink.
  3. Acceptance. "Instead of fighting the anxiety, let us learn to make space for it." Where is it in the body? "In my chest." "Let it be there. You do not have to remove it." (Eyes open is fine; closed eyes are optional.)
  4. Defusion. "Thoughts are not facts. They are words in your mind." The thought "I will embarrass myself" becomes "I am having the thought that I will embarrass myself." Said aloud many times, the sentence loses its grip.
  5. Present moment. "Let us focus on what is happening right now." The 5-4-3-2-1 grounding from {{page:tools}}, used in the minutes before a meeting.
  6. Values. "What kind of person do you want to be?" "I want to grow in my work." "Is skipping meetings helping you grow?"
  7. Committed action. "You can feel anxious and still act." A ladder of small steps: say one sentence, ask one question, share one idea.
  8. Practice between sessions. "Practice matters. Small steps make the change." Attend one meeting; say the defusion sentence once a day.
  9. Review. "It is all right if the anxiety is still there. What matters is what you do." The expected outcome is honest: the anxiety may remain, the behaviour changes, confidence follows.

  6. मूल्य। «तपाईं कस्तो व्यक्ति बन्न चाहनुहुन्छ?» «काममा अघि बढ्न चाहन्छु।» «मिटिङ छल्नुले अघि बढ्न सघाइरहेको छ?»
  7. प्रतिबद्ध कार्य। «चिन्ता भए पनि तपाईं काम गर्न सक्नुहुन्छ।» साना पाइलाको भर्‍याङ: एउटा वाक्य बोल्ने, एउटा प्रश्न सोध्ने, एउटा विचार राख्ने।
  8. सत्रबीचको अभ्यास। «अभ्यास महत्त्वपूर्ण छ। साना कदमले परिवर्तन ल्याउँछ।» एउटा मिटिङमा बस्ने; डिफ्युजन-वाक्य दिनमा एक पटक भन्ने।
  9. समीक्षा। «चिन्ता अझै भए पनि ठीक छ। महत्त्वपूर्ण कुरा तपाईंले के गर्नुहुन्छ भन्ने हो।» अपेक्षित नतिजा इमानदार छ: चिन्ता रहन सक्छ, व्यवहार बदलिन्छ, आत्मविश्वास पछि आउँछ।

Where it is used: anxiety disorders and depression have the best evidence. For OCD, ERP remains the first-line treatment and ACT is an addition to it; for trauma and substance use it is an adjunct, with a trained provider, not a stand-alone answer.

कहाँ प्रयोग हुन्छ: चिन्ता-विकार र डिप्रेसनमा सबैभन्दा राम्रो प्रमाण छ। ओसीडीमा ERP नै पहिलो उपचार हो र ACT त्यसमा थपिने विधि; आघात र लागुपदार्थमा यो प्रशिक्षित सेवाप्रदायकसँगको सहायक विधि हो, एक्लो उत्तर होइन।

Behavioural activation: action before motivationव्यवहार-सक्रियता: मन लाग्नुअघि नै काम

CTEVT PSC curriculumसीटीईभीटी पाठ्यक्रम

Depression makes a person do less; doing less makes the mood worse; the worse mood makes them do even less. That is the spiral on {{page:depression}}. Behavioural activation breaks it at the easiest link to move: the activity. The one line to remember is action before motivation: the client acts first, and the wish to act returns later. The class notes teach nine steps.

डिप्रेसनले मान्छेलाई कम गर्न लगाउँछ; कम गर्दा मन झन् खराब हुन्छ; खराब मनले झन् कम गराउँछ। त्यही {{पृष्ठ:depression}} को भुमरी हो। व्यवहार-सक्रियताले सबैभन्दा हल्लाउन सजिलो कडी, काम, समातेर भुमरी तोड्छ। सम्झने एउटै लाइन: मन लाग्नुअघि नै काम: सेवाग्राहीले पहिले गर्छ, गर्ने इच्छा पछि फर्कन्छ। कक्षा-नोटले नौ चरण सिकाउँछ।

  1. Understand the day. Sleep, meals, who they talk to, what they have stopped doing.
  2. Explain the cycle. "When we do less we feel worse, and when we feel worse we do less. We break it with action."
  3. Monitor activity. For a few days the client writes what they did each hour and how they felt.
  4. Schedule activity. A daily plan of small, realistic tasks. Not perfection, movement.
  5. Activate. Do the planned thing even when it does not feel like it. This step is the therapy.
  6. Add pleasure and achievement. One thing that is enjoyable, one that gives a sense of having done something.
  7. Solve the barriers. Tiredness, fear, no support: each named and worked around.
  8. Practise between sessions. This is where the change actually happens.
  9. Review. Every small gain counted out loud.
  1. दिन बुझ्ने। निद्रा, खाना, कोसँग बोल्छन्, के गर्न छोडे।

ERP: face the trigger, skip the ritualERP: ट्रिगरको सामना, रिचुअल छोड्ने

Exposure and response prevention (ERP) is the behavioural treatment for OCD. It rests on the loop drawn on {{page:ocd}}: trigger, obsession, anxiety, compulsion, short relief, and the loop tightened for next time. ERP breaks it at the compulsion. This is a treatment delivered by a trained person over weeks, with consent at every step; what follows is the shape of it, not a kit to run alone; the ladder below is one example a trained clinician built with one person after assessing real risk, not a template.

एक्सपोजर र प्रतिक्रिया-रोकथाम (ERP) ओसीडीको व्यवहारपरक उपचार हो। यो {{पृष्ठ:ocd}} मा कोरिएको चक्रमा अडिएको छ: ट्रिगर, अब्सेसन, चिन्ता, कम्पल्सन, छोटो राहत, अनि अर्को पटकका लागि झन् कसिएको चक्र। ERPले चक्र कम्पल्सनमै तोड्छ। यो प्रशिक्षित व्यक्तिले हप्तौंसम्म, हरेक चरणमा सहमति लिएर गर्ने उपचार हो; तल यसको आकार छ, एक्लै चलाउने किट होइन; तलको भर्‍याङ प्रशिक्षित चिकित्सकले वास्तविक जोखिम जाँचेर एक व्यक्तिसँग बनाएको एउटा उदाहरण हो, टेम्प्लेट होइन।

First, three things the client hears: an intrusive thought is frightening, but it is not an action and not a fact; the ritual calms for a minute and feeds a problem that can cost skin, sleep, work and mood for years.

पहिले, सेवाग्राहीले सुन्ने तीन कुरा: घुसपैठ गर्ने विचार डरलाग्दो हुन्छ, तर त्यो काम होइन, तथ्य पनि होइन; रिचुअलले एक मिनेट शान्त पार्छ र वर्षौंसम्म छाला, निद्रा, काम र मनस्थिति खाने समस्या खुवाउँछ।

Then a ladder. Together they list feared situations from easiest to hardest and rate each on SUDS, the 0 to 10 distress scale from {{page:tools}} (some services use 0 to 100; the idea is the same). Example for a fear of contamination:

The session. Start on the lowest rung. The client touches the table and stays with the feeling: "Stay with the anxiety. Do not wash your hands yet." "Notice the thoughts without acting on them." The aim is not to make the anxiety fall; it is to stay long enough to learn that the feared outcome is less likely, or more bearable, than the fear predicted, and that the anxiety, whether it falls or not, is survivable. Slow breathing may steady the body, but it is not used as an escape hatch from the feeling. SUDS is noted before, during and after, so the client can see their own record over the weeks.

सत्र। सबैभन्दा तल्लो खुड्किलोबाट सुरु। सेवाग्राही टेबल छुन्छ र भावनासँगै बस्छ: «चिन्तासँगै बस्नुहोस्। अहिले हात नधुनुहोस्।» «विचारलाई नमानी, हेरेर मात्र बस्नुहोस्।» लक्ष्य चिन्ता घटाउनु होइन; डराएको कुरा सोचेजति सम्भावित वा असह्य छैन रहेछ, र चिन्ता घटोस् वा नघटोस्, सहन सकिन्छ भन्ने सिक्न पुग्ने बेरसम्म बस्नु हो। बिस्तारै सासले शरीर स्थिर पार्न सक्छ, तर भावनाबाट भाग्ने ढोकाका रूपमा प्रयोग गरिँदैन। SUDS अघि, बीच र पछि लेखिन्छ, ताकि हप्तौंको आफ्नै अभिलेख सेवाग्राहीले देखोस्।

Response prevention is the principle that makes it ERP rather than just exposure: after the trigger, no washing, no sanitiser, no avoiding, or, where the clinician judges it right, an agreed delay that is then shortened. Practice is repeated and varied; the ladder moves up when the person has learned something new about the fear and feels ready, not when boredom sets in.

प्रतिक्रिया-रोकथाम त्यो सिद्धान्त हो जसले यसलाई एक्सपोजर मात्र होइन, ERP बनाउँछ: ट्रिगरपछि हात नधुने, स्यानिटाइजर नलगाउने, नछल्ने, वा चिकित्सकले उचित ठाने, सहमति गरेर राखिएको र क्रमशः घटाइने ढिलाइ। अभ्यास दोहोर्‍याइन्छ र फेरबदल गरिन्छ; व्यक्तिले डरबारे केही नयाँ सिकेपछि र तयार भएपछि भर्‍याङ माथि सर्छ, झ्याउ लागेपछि होइन।

For the counselor: never rush; always start low; consent and readiness before every rung; support the person warmly but do not reassure them about the fear itself ("you will be fine" is a ritual by proxy); keep the record. Alongside, gently test the thought: "If I do not wash I will get sick" becomes "Ordinary washing, before meals and after the toilet, continues; the extra washing is the OCD talking, and I can carry the not-knowing." Expect a stray intrusive thought to return now and then after treatment; the plan is not to go back to the ritual, and to keep the practice going.

परामर्शकर्ताका लागि: हतार कहिल्यै नगर्ने; सधैं तलबाट सुरु; हरेक खुड्किलोअघि सहमति र तयारी; व्यक्तिलाई न्यानो साथ दिने तर डरकै बारेमा ढुक्क नपार्ने («केही हुँदैन» भन्नु अरूमार्फतको रिचुअल हो); अभिलेख राख्ने। सँगसँगै विचारलाई नरम गरी जाँच्ने: «नधोए बिरामी हुन्छु» बाट «खानुअघि र शौचालयपछिको सामान्य धुवाइ चलिरहन्छ; थप धुवाइ ओसीडीको आवाज हो, र नजानेको अवस्था म बोक्न सक्छु।» उपचारपछि पनि कहिलेकाहीँ घुसपैठ-विचार फर्कन सक्छ भन्ने अपेक्षा राख्ने; योजना रिचुअलमा नफर्कने र अभ्यास जारी राख्ने हो।

Life skills: a map, not a new chapterजीवन-सीप: नक्सा, नयाँ अध्याय होइन

CTEVT PSC curriculumसीटीईभीटी पाठ्यक्रम

The class notes teach "life skills counselling" as a ten-step course; almost every step is already taught in full elsewhere on this site. The counselor's job is to find which skill is weak ("Which situations do you feel unable to handle?"), say plainly that skills are learned, not inborn, then teach, rehearse by role-play, set practice, and give feedback. Six areas:

कक्षा-नोटले «जीवन-सीप परामर्श» लाई दस चरणको कोर्सका रूपमा सिकाउँछ; झन्डै हरेक चरण यस साइटमा अन्यत्र पूरै सिकाइएको छ। परामर्शकर्ताको काम कुन सीप कमजोर छ भेट्नु («कुन अवस्था सम्हाल्न गाह्रो लाग्छ?»), सीप जन्मजात होइन, सिकिने हो भनेर प्रस्ट भन्नु, अनि सिकाउनु, रोल-प्लेले अभ्यास गराउनु, गृहकार्य दिनु र प्रतिक्रिया दिनु हो। छ क्षेत्र:

  • Emotional regulation: naming the feeling, slow breathing; the skill box below and {{page:tools}}.
  • Communication: I-statements ("I feel unheard when I speak" instead of "you never listen"), assertiveness; {{page:skills}}.
  • Problem solving: name it, list options, weigh them, choose one; brainstorming on {{page:skills-moving}}.
  • Decision making: listing pros and cons and thinking through consequences (the weighing step of brainstorming on {{page:skills-moving}}); assessed as task 12 on {{page:first-sessions}}.
  • Stress management: muscle relaxation, grounding, the ten habits; {{page:tools}} and {{page:wellbeing}}.
  • Social skills and routine: greeting, listening, keeping a daily shape to the day; {{page:basics}}.
  • भावना-नियमन: भावनालाई नाम दिने, बिस्तारै सास; तलको सीप-बाकस र {{पृष्ठ:tools}}।
  • संवाद: म-वाक्य («तिमी कहिल्यै सुन्दैनौ» होइन, «म बोल्दा नसुनिएको महसुस हुन्छ»), दृढता; {{पृष्ठ:skills}}।
  • समस्या-समाधान: नाम दिने, विकल्प सूची बनाउने, तौलने, एउटा रोज्ने; {{पृष्ठ:skills-moving}} को मन्थन।
  • निर्णय: फाइदा-बेफाइदा सूचीमा राख्ने र परिणाम सोच्ने ({{पृष्ठ:skills-moving}} को मन्थनमा तौलने चरण); {{पृष्ठ:first-sessions}} को कार्य १२ मा मूल्याङ्कन।
  • तनाव-व्यवस्थापन: मांसपेशी शिथिलीकरण, ग्राउन्डिङ, दस बानी; {{पृष्ठ:tools}} र {{पृष्ठ:wellbeing}}।
  • सामाजिक सीप र दैनिकी: अभिवादन, सुनाइ, दिनको आकार राख्ने; {{पृष्ठ:basics}}।
A skill to try · three minutesअभ्यास गर्ने सीप · तीन मिनेट

Five steps through a strong feeling. Notice early: feelings climb like a traffic light, green, yellow, red; catch it at yellow, the way you catch milk before it boils over. Your body gives the signal first: faster heart, quicker breath, a tight jaw. Find it: eyes open is fine; where does it sit, shoulders, chest, jaw? Let that place soften a little. Name it: anger is often a shield; ask "is this anger, or is there hurt, fear or shame underneath?" A named feeling loses some of its force. Breathe out long: in through the nose, out slowly and a little longer than in; no holding, no counting contest, stop if it feels wrong. Ask what it needs: turn it into an I-message: "I feel ___ because I needed ___; next time, could you ___?" Use this only where it is safe to speak plainly; where there is violence or control at home, safety comes first ({{page:gbv}}). Later, if it helps, write down what happened, what you felt and what you needed, facts first, blame left out.

तीव्र भावनाबाट पाँच पाइलामा। चाँडै थाहा पाउने: भावना ट्राफिक बत्तीजस्तै चढ्छ, हरियो, पहेँलो, रातो; पहेँलोमै समात्ने, उम्लन लागेको दूध पोखिनुअघि समातेजस्तै। शरीरले पहिले सङ्केत दिन्छ: मुटु छिटो, सास छिटो, बङ्गारा कसिएको। खोज्ने: आँखा खुला राखे हुन्छ; कहाँ बसेको छ, काँध, छाती, बङ्गारा? त्यो ठाउँ अलिकति खुकुलो हुन दिने। नाम दिने: रिस प्रायः ढाल हो; सोध्ने, «यो रिस हो कि भित्र चोट, डर वा लाज छ?» नाम पाएको भावनाले केही शक्ति गुमाउँछ। लामो सास छोड्ने: नाकबाट तान्ने, बिस्तारै र तान्दाभन्दा अलि लामो छोड्ने; सास नरोक्ने, गन्तीको प्रतिस्पर्धा नगर्ने, असजिलो लागे रोक्ने। के चाहिएको हो सोध्ने: म-वाक्यमा ढाल्ने: «मलाई ___ लाग्यो किनभने मलाई ___ चाहिएको थियो; अर्को पटक ___ गर्न सकिन्छ?» यो सुरक्षित ठाउँमा मात्र; घरमा हिंसा वा नियन्त्रण छ भने पहिले सुरक्षा ({{पृष्ठ:gbv}})। पछि, काम लाग्छ भने, के भयो, के लाग्यो, के चाहिएको थियो लेख्ने, पहिले तथ्य, दोष छोडेर।

A coping skill, not a treatment: it supports care, it does not replace it.यो सामना गर्ने सीप हो, उपचार होइन: यसले हेरचाहलाई सघाउँछ, प्रतिस्थापन गर्दैन।
