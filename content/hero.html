A plain-language map of mental disorders, open to anyone learning: DSM-5 diagnostic criteria, teaching diagrams, international examples, and the Nepali context for each. Topics from the CTEVT Psychosocial Counselor curriculum carry a small tag, so counselling students can see at a glance what is examinable.

What the two tags mean: every chapter and card carries one. CTEVT PSC curriculum marks content that is on the official Psychosocial Counselor syllabus and can appear in the exam. Beyond curriculum marks content added for completeness (the rest of the DSM-5, newer research, practical extras): worth knowing, not examinable. Readers who are not students can ignore the tags.

दुई ट्यागको अर्थ: हरेक अध्याय र कार्डमा एउटा हुन्छ। सीटीईभीटी पाठ्यक्रम ले आधिकारिक मनोसामाजिक परामर्शकर्ता पाठ्यक्रमभित्र पर्ने, परीक्षामा आउन सक्ने सामग्री जनाउँछ। पाठ्यक्रमभन्दा बाहिर ले पूर्णताका लागि थपिएको सामग्री जनाउँछ (DSM-5 का बाँकी भाग, नयाँ अनुसन्धान, व्यावहारिक थप): जान्न लायक, तर परीक्षामा नआउने। विद्यार्थी नभएका पाठकले ट्यागलाई बेवास्ता गरे हुन्छ।

If you or someone near you is struggling right now: in Nepal, call the National Suicide Prevention Helpline 1166 (Mental Hospital, Lagankhel) or the TUTH mental-health hotline 1660 012 1600; in an emergency dial 112 or 100. This site is for learning: it cannot replace a person who can help.
