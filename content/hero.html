A plain-language map of mental disorders, open to anyone learning: DSM-5 diagnostic criteria, teaching diagrams, international examples, and the Nepali context for each. Topics from the CTEVT Psychosocial Counselor curriculum carry a small tag, so counselling students can see at a glance what is examinable.
